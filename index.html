About Us

We are an Assembly of Christians who believe and teach that the Holy Bible is the Word of God, that the Lord Jesus Christ is the Son of God, and that eternal salvation is available to all who trust the work of Christ accomplished on the cross of Calvary.

We're glad you stopped by. If you have any questions or would like to know more, please feel free to contact us. After you've looked around our site, why not come and visit us at 3730 O'Hare Drive, Mesquite, TX 75150?
